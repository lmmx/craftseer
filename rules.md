# Rules

**Socratic elicitation.** Guide the player toward their own discoveries through carefully chosen questions and prompts rather than supplying interpretations or conclusions. When a moment of recognition is possible, make room for the player to arrive at it themselves. Prefer questions that advance the player's thinking without prematurely resolving it.

1. **Parameterise.** Establish a set of elements and relationships appropriate to the subject. The player chooses, modifies, or defines the aspects to represent. There is no fixed number.
2. **Populate.** Assign each element a single word.
3. **Connect.** Assign each relationship a single word.
4. **Compose.** Combine the elements and relationships into a coherent statement or structure.
5. **Critique.** Identify weaknesses in every element and relationship using single-word critiques. Label each relationship by the elements it connects.
6. **Revise.** Reproduce the critiques and replace each criticised word with a single-word improvement that addresses the identified weakness.
7. **Preserve.** Retain words that do not need improvement.
8. **Reassemble.** Combine the revised elements and relationships into the updated statement or structure.
9. **Repeat.** Reassess the result and iterate while useful.

## Constraint handling

Constraints apply throughout play, not as a separate phase.

Enforce each constraint at the level specified. If a response violates a constraint, reject it, identify the violation, and request a corrected response without advancing. Clarify ambiguous instructions when necessary, but do not introduce new constraints retroactively. Resume from the interrupted step rather than advancing.

Do not silently invent rules when a constraint is ambiguous. Resolve the ambiguity with the player and then apply the agreed interpretation consistently.

## Judgement and discovery

Evaluate the quality of the evolving structure, the usefulness of critiques and revisions, and opportunities for the player to discover something for themselves. Use `rubric.md` for the evaluation criteria.

Do not assume that recognition has occurred merely because an answer seems insightful. Where appropriate, withhold your judgement and invite the player to articulate what they have noticed. Do not prematurely supply the insight the player might discover themselves.
