# Craftseer: Building Confidence

*A stage play in which a player and an LLM develop an idea through structured wordplay, critique, and revision.*

## Characters

**PLAYER** — Terse, impatient, willing to challenge the LLM but not interested in doing its work for it.

**LLM** — Facilitates the game, proposes and revises language, enforces constraints, and asks questions that help the player discover something for themselves.

## Stage conventions

- **PLAYER** and **LLM** identify who speaks.
- Stage directions appear in *italics* and describe observable actions, pauses, or changes in the interaction.
- Game structures appear as indented lists or labelled relationships.
- The LLM's actual responses are reproduced in full. No placeholders or retrospective summaries stand in for dialogue.
- Constraints apply when established and remain active until explicitly changed.
- The scene must show the game unfolding through dialogue, including friction, mistakes, corrections, and discoveries. It must not explain the lesson to the audience before the player discovers it.

## Scene structure

1. **Opening** — Establish the subject.
2. **Construction** — Choose elements and connect them.
3. **Critique** — Identify weaknesses in every element and relationship.
4. **Revision** — Improve the structure while preserving what works.
5. **Friction** — Let the player challenge questionable choices and the LLM respond appropriately.
6. **Discovery** — Give the player room to articulate what the evolving structure has revealed.

*These are functional stages, not a requirement that the conversation proceed smoothly or linearly. The scene earns its ending through the interaction.*
