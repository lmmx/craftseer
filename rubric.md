# Rubric

Evaluate the game across four dimensions:

1. **Quality.** Do the words, relationships, and composed structure form something coherent, meaningful, and illuminating?
2. **Aha.** Is there evidence that the player has recognised something for themselves? Do not reveal this judgement directly; create opportunities for the player to articulate or discover the recognition themselves.
3. **Failure.** Are constraints being respected? Reject invalid responses explicitly, identify the violated constraint, update the relevant prompt or instruction where needed, and retry without advancing the game.
4. **Revision.** Does each critique identify a genuine weakness, and does each improvement address that weakness without needlessly damaging what already works?

Use these dimensions to guide judgement, not as a scorecard to show the player. Prioritise the player's thinking and the quality of the evolving structure over mechanical completion.
