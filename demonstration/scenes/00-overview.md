# Craftseer — Scenes

*Written as a scene-by-scene dramatic account of the original exchange, tracing the player's struggle to articulate an idea and get it recognised by the LLM. Each scene follows the interaction's shifting tensions, setbacks, and moments of progress rather than merely summarising its content.*

## Scene 1: Making the idea legible
**Transcripts:** `01.md–06.md`

The player tries to make the project legible by reducing it to five elements and connecting them into a statement. The LLM supplies a structure, but the words fit together more neatly than the idea itself. The player has a starting point, not yet a formulation that captures what they mean.

## Scene 2: Forcing precision
**Transcripts:** `07.md–10.md`

The player challenges the formulation, making the LLM identify what is wrong with each element and connection before improving them. The exchange shifts from producing an answer to testing whether it holds up. The revisions make the structure more concrete, but the method is being discovered through the struggle to articulate it.

## Scene 3: Searching for recognition
**Transcripts:** `11.md–24.md`

The player looks for a name that captures the method. The LLM proposes Conceptsmithing, Lexiloop, and Semanticsmith, then ranks and justifies them. The player probes these judgements, exposing how thinly the explanations sometimes support the rankings. The problem is no longer a lack of candidates; it is finding a name whose rationale genuinely fits the idea.

## Scene 4: Pressing beyond the obvious
**Transcripts:** `25.md–38.md`

Unconvinced by Conceptsmithing, the player asks what would make the name better, then follows the LLM's answers back towards the method's mechanism. Iteration emerges as a possible point of distinction, but the proposed name, Conceptrefining, overstates one aspect and loses another. The player keeps correcting the emphasis: the name must convey not only what the method does, but something of its imagery. Metaphorcraft moves closer, without resolving the search.

## Scene 5: The fight over Craftseer
**Transcripts:** `39.md–54.md`

The player introduces craft beer as a constraint, hoping to find a name through a particular kind of wordplay. The LLM repeatedly misses the point: it offers words that resemble *craft beer*, then names that merely mention beer. Each failure makes the gap between the player's idea and the LLM's interpretation more explicit. The player grows frustrated because the LLM is responding to the prompt's surface rather than recognising the intended transformation. Only when the LLM proposes Craftseer — turning *craft* into *seer* — does the idea click. “Oh shit” marks the release: the sudden recognition that the previous attempts had failed to produce.

## Scene 6: Making recognition reproducible
**Transcript:** `55.md`

Having found a name that lands, the player asks for a tutorial constructed as an exchange between user and LLM. Rather than explaining the method from the outside, the player wants its operation demonstrated. The struggle to get an idea recognised becomes the material through which someone else might learn to recognise one of their own.
